Tells — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

The short version: Tells has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of ours that store your personal information. Your slips, preferences, and profile photo live on your device. Nothing is sold to anyone.

1. Who we are

Tells ("the app") is an independent iOS sports companion — live scores, sports news, and personal bet-slip tracking — developed by Andrew Klotz ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

2. Information stored on your device

The app does not require an account. The following stays on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Bet slips you enter or scan, and their settlement history
  • Teams you follow, display name, notification preferences, sportsbook preference, and default stake
  • Your profile photo, if you set one

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also remove individual items in the app at any time (for example, deleting slips or removing your photo).

3. Information that leaves your device

To do its job, the app talks directly to a small number of third-party services. As with any internet connection, those services can see standard connection data (such as your IP address). The app sends them no name, account, or advertising identifier:

  • The Odds API — requests for live scores, schedules, and betting odds. Requests describe games and sports, not you.
  • ESPN, CBS Sports, and BettingPros — sports news articles, team logos, and images are fetched from their servers.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — only when you use slip scanning (the slip image you select is sent to extract bet details) or article summaries (article text is sent to generate the summary). We do not store these on any server of ours.
  • Google's favicon service — small sportsbook logo images.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Photos

Photos are accessed through the system photo picker, which runs outside the app — the app only ever receives the specific images you choose (a slip to scan, or a profile photo). Your profile photo is stored on your device only.

5. No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data for marketing. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Children

The app is not directed at individuals under 17, and its betting-related features are intended only for people of legal sports-wagering age in their jurisdiction.

7. Your choices

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete slips, unfollow teams, remove your photo, or delete the app to remove everything. Questions about this policy are welcome at the contact below.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example, if a future update adds accounts or sync — this page will be updated and the effective date revised before those changes ship.
